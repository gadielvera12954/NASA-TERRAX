# Surface brief · TERRA-X web app (all screens)

Scope: the whole MVP app (web/src/sistema/MvpApp.tsx and the screens it mounts). Mode: Persuade on Home (jury demo), Operate on Explore, Card, Lab, Verification and Guide, all inside one world.
Audience: NASA Space Apps jury on a projector; secondary public and scientists. Action: "Explore a candidate". Proof: real LOLA relief in 3D, real candidates with C/E, sources with SHA-256. Constraints: never invent numbers; English default with Spanish toggle; local copy, not pushed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a sunlit wall that reads the Sun from the shadows it casts. It refuses the dark-space hero with a glowing planet and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-plaster ground (#E4E2DC), carbon ink (#141414), iron-oxide meridian red (#A8321E) as the only accent, ochre plaster (#C98F55) for Earth fields, basalt plates (#232322) for everything lunar. Expanded heavy Archivo headlines, Public Sans text, Azeret Mono for every measured number. Square plates, 1px ink rules, hard shadows with no blur driven by the Sun variables.

STORY: The visitor sees the Sun is low, sees long shadows, understands that grazing light and long night are the lunar problem, then opens a real Earth candidate and reads its limits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a live mono readout of the Sun and the board shadow, a huge expanded headline casting a long hard shadow, a two-line lede and an ink plaque "Explore a candidate" with an oxide rule. Right, the Mons Mouton relief set in as a basalt plate with hairline labels. Bottom, a horizon rule and the Sun scale 1.5° to 10° as the gnomon control; moving it lengthens every shadow on the page.

FORM: Meridian Wall, position 5 of the grounded list (sundials and meridian lines), seed:1e35e5898ed08eb6. Raises: threshold horizon with night held as unmarked dark (accretion disk); one measurement per full-width row (forging); hairline labels on every object (busytown).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
